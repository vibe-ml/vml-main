# Emerging Technology Intelligence

A glossary for identifying early scientific and technological developments that innovation analysts can assess for potential banking applications. Terminology follows the [problem statement](docs/problem/gpb-problem-detailed.md), [expert Q&A](docs/problem/q&a/q&a-2026-09-17-summary.md), and [research report](docs/agents/research/trends/trends-01-research.md); research proposals remain provisional where expert agreement is unresolved.

## Language

### Search and technology boundaries

**Innovation analyst — Аналитик инноваций**:
The user who evaluates technological evidence and potential business applications, including opportunities for new banking products.

**Technology domain — Технологическое направление**:
A broad field that defines the search scope, such as microfluidics, AI security, or 3D printing.
_Avoid_: Trend or weak signal for an entire field.

**Open query — Открытый запрос**:
A free-form request to find early technologies within a domain, beyond the supplied examples or a fixed technology catalog.

**Search profile — Поисковый профиль**:
The interpretation of an open query through relevant concepts, aliases, classification terms, time boundaries, and exclusions.

**Technology branch — Технологическая ветвь**:
A specific method, component, or application with its own development evidence; its maturity can differ from its parent technology.

**Technology alias — Вариант названия технологии**:
An alternative name, abbreviation, spelling, or translation that refers to the same technology branch.

**Technology candidate — Технология-кандидат**:
A technology branch under assessment for weak-signal status; discovery alone does not establish eligibility for the final results.
_Avoid_: Confirmed weak signal for an unassessed candidate.

**Evidence cluster — Кластер свидетельств**:
A group of documents or observations about the same technology branch, including mentions under different aliases.

### Signals and development stages

**Weak signal — Слабый сигнал**:
An early, uncertain indication of a technology branch supported by scientific or patent activity, with little mass attention and no established maturity or overheating.
The project's working target follows the Q&A's TRL 1–3 orientation; a weak signal is not a prediction of commercial success.
_Avoid_: Little-known technology as sufficient grounds for this status.

**Emerging trend — Зарождающийся тренд**:
A developing pattern of related weak signals with persistence and independent corroboration, rather than an isolated observation.
In this project, final emerging-trend results also meet the early-stage criteria for weak signals.

**Growing trend — Растущий тренд**:
A technology direction gaining substantial visibility or adoption beyond the target weak-signal stage; rapid growth alone does not justify inclusion.

**Mature trend — Зрелый тренд**:
A technology direction with widespread adoption, an established market, dominant participants, or established industry standards.
_Avoid_: Weak signal merely because local media coverage is low.

**Technology readiness level (TRL) — Уровень готовности технологии (УГТ)**:
An evidence-based assessment of technical development; the project's early-stage orientation covers principles, concepts, and proof of concept at TRL 1–3.
A patent, funding-round label, or informal development-stage description alone does not establish TRL.

**Adoption evidence — Свидетельства внедрения**:
Documented use of a technology in practice, distinguished from research demonstrations, pilots, and announced plans.

**Market maturity — Зрелость рынка**:
The extent to which a technology has an established market, identifiable leaders, and stable competitive structure.

**Investment overheating — Инвестиционный перегрев**:
Mass venture investment indicating that a technology has moved beyond the project's target early stage; quantitative thresholds remain unresolved.
Research grants and funding of a parent platform do not automatically establish overheating of a specific branch.

**Investment status — Инвестиционный статус**:
An assessment of overheating, no overheating found within the checked scope, or unknown investment conditions.
No discovered funding round does not establish an absence of investment.

**Marketing hype — Маркетинговый хайп**:
Promotional attention that exaggerates or substitutes for evidence of technological progress or practical value.

**Information noise — Информационный шум**:
Irrelevant, repetitive, or unsupported material that does not substantiate a candidate's technological development.

### Sources, evidence, and time

**Source — Источник**:
An identifiable publication, record, or other material used in assessment, with its title, link, publication date, type, language, and trust assessment.

**Primary source — Первоисточник**:
The original material reporting a result, filing, or announcement; being original does not establish independent verification of every claim.

**Claim — Утверждение**:
A checkable statement about a candidate, such as its demonstrated capability, development stage, investment, or adoption.

**Evidence — Свидетельство**:
A traceable source passage or record supporting or contradicting a specific claim about a candidate.
_Avoid_: Link alone as proof; generated summary as a primary source.

**Independent corroboration — Независимое подтверждение**:
Support originating independently of the claim's initial publication; reposts of one announcement do not provide independent corroboration.

**Evidence provenance — Происхождение свидетельства**:
The traceable origin and publication history connecting a claim to its underlying material, including reused announcements and translated passages.

**Source trust — Доверенность источника**:
The assessed reliability of a source for a particular claim; authority on patents does not establish authority on investment absence.

**Source coverage — Охват источников**:
The sources, languages, regions, and periods actually examined, together with known gaps that limit conclusions.

**Collection scope — Область сбора**:
The scientific works selected for collection by publication period and any configured domain or field restrictions. An unspecified domain or field restriction does not exclude works on that basis.

**Corpus freshness — Актуальность корпуса**:
How recently the collected scientific records were checked against their source, stated for the portions actually checked. A recent collection run does not establish that the entire corpus is current.

**Patent family — Патентное семейство**:
A group of related patent filings covering the same invention, treated as related evidence rather than independent inventions.

**Analysis cutoff — Дата среза анализа**:
The latest date at which information may have been available for a given assessment; later knowledge belongs to a later assessment.

**Candidate snapshot — Срез состояния кандидата**:
A candidate's evidence, development assessment, and classification at a specified analysis cutoff.

**Earliest observed mention — Самое раннее найденное упоминание**:
The earliest reliable mention within the examined corpus and known aliases, not necessarily the first occurrence worldwide.

**Planned event — Планируемое событие**:
A future activity described in an available announcement, distinct from evidence that the activity has already occurred.

**Publication lag — Задержка публикации**:
The interval between an underlying activity and public disclosure, which limits conclusions from missing recent publications or patents.

**Evidence conflict — Противоречие свидетельств**:
Incompatible claims about the same candidate and scope; source rank alone does not settle the candidate's weak-signal status.
The Q&A's automatic classification rule and the research proposal for review remain unreconciled.

### Assessment and ranking

**Emergence indicator — Признак зарождения**:
An observable property used to assess early development, such as novelty, scientific activity, persistence, or limited mass attention.
_Avoid_: Proof of emergence for any single indicator.

**Novelty — Новизна**:
The distinctness of a technology branch or combination of methods and applications relative to earlier evidence in the examined domain.

**Scientific and patent activity — Научно-патентная активность**:
Research results and patent-family activity associated with a candidate over an identified period.

**Mention dynamics — Динамика упоминаний**:
Changes in a candidate's document counts and share within a comparable domain, source group, and period.

**Persistence — Устойчивость сигнала**:
Continued evidence of a technology branch across successive periods, beyond a short-lived burst of attention.

**Cross-source convergence — Межисточниковая конвергенция**:
Independent evidence from different source types pointing to the same developing technology branch.

**Mass attention — Массовое внимание**:
Broad public or marketing visibility, distinguished from early mentions in specialist scientific and technical communities.

**Eligibility — Допустимость включения в выдачу**:
Whether a candidate meets the early-stage, relevance, and evidence criteria for final results, independently of its ranking score.

**Needs review — Требует проверки**:
A provisional assessment for insufficient or conflicting evidence, unclear technology boundaries, or uncertain readiness.
Its use as a separate status follows the research proposal and does not imply a confirmed positive or negative class.

**Exclusion reason — Причина исключения**:
An evidence-based explanation for omitting a candidate, such as maturity, overheating, irrelevance, or marketing noise.

**Ranking score — Балл ранжирования**:
A measure of relative priority among eligible candidates; it does not express the probability of correct classification.
_Avoid_: Confidence percentage for an uncalibrated ranking score.

**Evidence sufficiency — Достаточность свидетельств**:
The extent to which available, independent evidence and source coverage support an assessment; this is separate from model confidence.

**Model confidence — Уверенность модели**:
The model's assessed confidence in weak-signal classification for a candidate snapshot; a probability interpretation requires independent calibration validation.
_Avoid_: Ranking score or supplied dataset score as a synonym.

**Classification rationale — Обоснование классификации**:
An explanation connecting a candidate's status to observed indicators, supporting and opposing evidence, and remaining uncertainty.

### Analyst results and validation

**Top-15 results — Выдача ТОП-15**:
The ranked set of 15 eligible early technology candidates for an open query; fewer eligible results constitute an incomplete result set.

**Insight — Инсайт**:
An evidence-grounded analytical account of a candidate's technology, potential advantage, case example, classification rationale, and sources.

**Potential advantage — Потенциальное преимущество**:
The expected benefit of a technology for a stated problem, with demonstrated results distinguished from anticipated gains.

**Case example — Кейс-пример**:
A documented research or practical example illustrating the technology; it need not represent commercial or mass adoption.

**Business application hypothesis — Гипотеза бизнес-применения**:
A proposed use of a technology for banking or another business need, explicitly distinguished from an observed case.

**Expert validation — Экспертная валидация**:
An analyst's or jury member's assessment of signal status, evidence quality, novelty, and practical relevance, including reasons for disagreement.

**Provided annotation — Предоставленная аннотация**:
A supplied example's author-written stage, explanation, mention trend, or score, distinct from verified evidence and independent reference labels.

**Reference label — Эталонная метка**:
An independently established classification for a candidate snapshot under defined criteria, used to assess detection quality.
_Avoid_: Ground truth for an unverified example annotation or model-generated judgment.

**Detection precision — Точность выявления (Precision)**:
The proportion of predicted weak signals accepted as weak signals by the reference assessment.
It differs from accuracy, which measures correct classifications across all evaluated classes.

**Detection recall — Полнота выявления (Recall)**:
The proportion of reference weak signals found by the system; for open search, this is limited to a defined reference set.

**Detection F1 score — F1-мера выявления**:
The harmonic mean of detection precision and recall against reference labels, balancing false positives and missed signals.

**Precision at 15 — Точность первых 15 результатов (Precision@15)**:
The proportion of a complete Top-15 result set accepted by independent expert assessment; shorter lists have a different denominator.
